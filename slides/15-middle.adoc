== The uncomfortable middle

++++
<p class="fragment" data-fragment-index="0">
  It's not "AI replaces devs".<br/>
  It's not "AI is useless hype".
</p>
<p class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1">
  For years we worked to raise the ceiling.<br/>
  AI didn't raise the ceiling. <strong>It blew the roof off.</strong>
</p>
<p class="fragment" data-fragment-index="4" style="color:#555; font-style:italic;">
  Turns out that wasn't entirely comforting.
</p>
