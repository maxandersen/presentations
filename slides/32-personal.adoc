== I was the Enthusiast

[%step]
* I got excited. _Really_ excited.
* I rebuilt workflows. I shipped faster. I felt like I'd unlocked a cheat code.
* I assumed my team would feel the same — that _most_ developers would
* They didn't

[.fragment]
Some were cautious. Some were quiet. Some pushed back. +
And I couldn't understand why.

[.fragment]
It took me longer than I'd like to admit to realise: +
_I was so caught up in what the tools could do, I forgot to ask what the people around me were feeling._

[NOTE.speaker]
--
This is the honest moment. You were the Enthusiast — own it.
The insight is not "AI is great" or "AI is scary."
It's: excitement can make you blind to the people next to you.
That's what led to this talk.
--
