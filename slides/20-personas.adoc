== The Enthusiast

[.columns]
=== !

[.column]
--
image::personas/1-enthusiast.png[The Enthusiast,width=85%]
--

[.column]
--
[.persona.enthusiast]
"I ship 3x more. I can finally build the side projects I dreamed about. Why is anyone still typing by hand?"

[%step]
* *Strength:* leverage, exploration, momentum
* *Risk:* shipping code they don't understand
* *Risk:* atrophy of skills they'll need when the model is wrong
* *Risk:* mistaking *output* for *value*
--

[NOTE.speaker]
--
Lead-in (spoken, not on slide): "I want to introduce you to four developers I keep meeting.
Composites of real conversations. See who you recognise — including in the mirror."

Then on this slide: don't mock the Enthusiast. They're often right about productivity.
But "I shipped it" ≠ "it works" ≠ "it's maintainable".
--

== The Skeptic

[.columns]
=== !

[.column]
--
image::personas/2-skeptic.png[The Skeptic,width=85%]
--

[.column]
--
[.persona.skeptic]
"It hallucinates. It writes plausible nonsense. I spent more time fixing it than writing it myself. The craft matters."

[%step]
* *Strength:* taste, rigor, defending quality
* *Risk:* judging today's tools by last year's experience
* *Risk:* becoming the person the team routes *around*
* *Risk:* confusing "I don't like it" with "it doesn't work"
--

[NOTE.speaker]
--
The skeptic is often the best engineer in the room.
Their concerns are real. But the tools moved fast.
"I tried it in 2023" is not a current data point.
--

== The Pragmatist

[.columns]
=== !

[.column]
--
image::personas/3-pragmatist.png[The Pragmatist,width=85%]
--

[.column]
--
[.persona.pragmatist]
"I use it for the boring stuff. Tests, scaffolding, that regex I always forget. I don't use it for the parts I actually care about."

[%step]
* *Strength:* clear sense of where it helps
* *Strength:* still owns the architecture and the hard calls
* *Risk:* the "boring stuff" line keeps moving
* *Risk:* underestimating how the tool changes *what* boring means
--

[NOTE.speaker]
--
This is where most experienced devs end up — for now.
But the boundary is not static. Re-evaluate quarterly, not yearly.
--

== The Quietly Lost

[.columns]
=== !

[.column]
--
image::personas/4-quietly-lost.png[The Quietly Lost,width=85%]
--

[.column]
--
[.persona.lost]
"Everyone on my team is doing this. I nod in standup. I don't actually know what tools they use or whether I'm falling behind."

[%step]
* *Most common reaction I see*
* *Risk:* silence becomes a career problem
* *Risk:* shame stops them asking
* *The room is bigger than they think*
--

[NOTE.speaker]
--
Be gentle here. This is the largest group and the most invisible one.
Encourage: ask in your team. You are not the only one.
--
