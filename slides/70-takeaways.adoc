== What I'm asking you to do

Not "use AI". +
Not "don't use AI". +
*Understand it.*

[%step]
* Try the tools your team uses — at least enough to review them
* Form an opinion based on *this year*, not 2023
* Notice what you actually identify with in your work
* Talk about it with your team, not just on Twitter

== A short checklist

[%step]
. Use a current AI coding tool on a real task this week
. Review one AI-generated PR with full attention
. Ask one teammate how *they* use it
. Write down one thing it did well, one thing it did badly
. Decide what you *won't* delegate — and why
