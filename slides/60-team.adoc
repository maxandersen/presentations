== This is a team problem, not a personal one

[%step]
* Different people on your team are at different stages
* Most teams have *no shared agreement* on how AI is used
* That silence is where the friction lives
* Reviews get weird. Trust gets weird. Estimates get weird.

[NOTE.speaker]
--
Pivot from individual to team. This is the section managers will quote.
--

== Questions to actually ask your team

[%step]
* Who uses what, for what?
* Do we disclose AI-generated PRs? Should we?
* What does "I reviewed it" mean now?
* What can a junior *not* learn if AI does it for them?
* Where are we accepting code we don't understand?

[.fragment]
You don't need answers. +
You need the *conversation*.

[NOTE.speaker]
--
Encourage the audience to start this conversation Monday morning.
Concrete prompt: pick one of these and bring it to standup.
--

== Where you and your team stand

[.fragment]
There is no single right answer.

[%step]
* A safety-critical team is not a startup
* A 2-person team is not a 200-person team
* A greenfield project is not a 15-year monolith
* Your context decides — but you have to *decide*

[NOTE.speaker]
--
Resist prescribing a single policy. The point is intentionality.
--
