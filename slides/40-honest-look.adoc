== What AI is good at — and isn't

[.columns]
=== !

[.column]
--
.Good at (today)
* Boilerplate, scaffolding, glue
* "How do I do X in lib Y"
* Tests you would have skipped
* Renames, refactors, conversions
* Explaining unfamiliar code
* First drafts of almost anything
--

[.column]
--
.Still bad at (today)
* Knowing what it doesn't know
* Long-range consistency
* Your team's unwritten conventions
* Saying "don't build this"
* Being accountable
--

[.fragment]
The bad-at list is *exactly* the senior-engineer list.

[NOTE.speaker]
--
Land both halves, then the punchline. Slow down on the last line — this is what skeptics need to hear.
Their value didn't disappear, it got more concentrated.
